Lemmatization: a Natural Language Processing (NLP) technique that reduces words to their dictionary-recognized base or root form, known as a lemma, by considering their meaning and part of speech within a sentence. Unlike stemming, which simply chops off word endings, lemmatization uses a vocabulary and morphological analysis to ensure the resulting lemma is a valid word, improving text preprocessing for applications like search engines, chatbots, and sentiment analysis
		Stemming
			is a cruder method that simply chops off word endings to get a "stem". For example, "running" might become "run," but "better" might become "better" or "bet."
		Lemmatization
			is more linguistically sophisticated. For example, "running" becomes "run," and "better" correctly becomes "good".
